# Rain

> Rain is an enterprise stablecoin payments platform that lets companies embed digital-dollar money movement into their products through a single API. It spans stablecoin wallets and virtual accounts, card issuing (Rain is a Visa and Mastercard Principal Member, settling Visa card transactions daily in stablecoins across multiple blockchains), and money movement via fiat on-ramps, off-ramps, and cross-border payment orchestration. Rain maintains PCI DSS and SOC 2 compliance.
